# Privacy policy for RetroLink

_Last updated: <date>_

RetroLink is an emulator. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data that stays on your device

Your games, save files, save states and settings are stored on your device only.

## Online rooms

When you use the Online tab, the app connects to the RetroLink room server at
`<your server address>`. The server receives:

- the display name you enter,
- the name of the game you host, the room settings and the room code,
- the link-cable or controller data exchanged during the game,
- your IP address, which every internet connection reveals.

Room data is kept only in memory while the room is open and is deleted when the room
closes. Reports you send about a room are written to the server log to deal with abuse.
Your games and save files are never uploaded.

## Cloud save backup (optional)

If you turn on save sync, the app stores your saves in a private app folder in your own
Google Drive. We cannot see this folder.
